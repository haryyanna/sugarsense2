Cookbook/Recipes is now available in Android Cookbook/Recipes have been a long requested feature from Android users and has been a very obvious gap between the Android and iOS versions of our apps. We’re happy to say that with this release we have FINALLY closed that gap. Android users can now create new recipes, browse public recipes from other users, and save recipes to their food diary, just like on the website or on an iPhone.
