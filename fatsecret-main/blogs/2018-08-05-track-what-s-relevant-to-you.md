Custom Meal Headings For many years, our users have been requesting the ability to have more meal segments beyond the three core meals and snacks. We’re pleased to announce that an additional six meal types can be added to your diary on top of the existing four. You have control over which ones are, or are not visible, and you can customise what you call them, so that their names make sense to you.
