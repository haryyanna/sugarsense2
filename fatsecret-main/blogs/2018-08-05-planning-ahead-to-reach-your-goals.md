We’ve spent the past year conducting research with our fatsecret users to get a better understanding of what their challenges are when it comes to staying on track. We know that when people decide on impulse to get on a diet, get fit, lose weight, they do so with the best of intentions. Unfortunately, “winging it” is the fastest way to failure.
