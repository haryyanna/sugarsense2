Low hanging fruit and closing the loop As we’ve been building new features into the app in 2017, and preparing for the year(s) ahead, we’ve been talking to our users a bit more than usual, both via in-app surveys and a lengthy user research project. We’ve started delving into the backlog of feature requests for our users and we’re now delivering the features that we think are the highest value. The ability to sync up with your favourite exercise tracker was one, being able to set reminders was another.
